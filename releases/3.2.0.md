# AutoTrim Exporter 3.2.0

AutoTrim Exporter 3.2.0 puts the trimming decision back in your hands and fixes
every case we found where the exporter removed audio you wanted to keep. You can
now switch trimming off completely, and decide how many render passes a delivery
is allowed to use. Rendering engine 2.9.0.

## New

- **No trim.** Deliver the selection exactly as it sits on the timeline. No
  boundary detection, no pre-roll, no tail padding, no fades — one render.
  Choose it under **Trim & Tails › Trimming**.
- **Render passes.** Decide how much work an export is allowed to do:
  - **1 · deliver only** — one render per file. Nothing is analysed, so nothing
    is trimmed. The fastest possible export.
  - **2 · analyse + deliver** — one analysis render finds the boundaries within
    a five-second tail scan, then the delivery renders.
  - **3 · extended tail** — a second analysis render may scan up to thirty
    seconds when a long reverb tail is still ringing.
- With two passes, a tail that is still sounding at the end of the scan is
  reported in the run details instead of failing the export.

## Fixed — audio that used to be lost

- **Fades no longer ramp across your sound.** The fade-out is limited to the
  silence the delivery actually gained after the detected audio, and the
  per-item fade-in to the pre-roll actually gained. Before this release, a
  tightly edited one-shot whose audio ran to the edge of the item still received
  the full 150 ms safety-ending fade — ramping the last 150 ms of the asset down
  to silence. The same happened to long reverbs when the thirty-second scan
  ceiling cut the tail short.
- **Slow swells are not trimmed.** A crescendo has no steady noise floor to
  learn from. The learned floor is now only trusted when the quiet part of the
  material is genuinely stationary, so the quiet beginning of a rising sound
  survives.
- **Events surrounded by digital silence keep their attack.** A soft attack next
  to true silence was being learned as "noise" and cut.
- **Impulsive tails survive.** Clicks, debris, rattles and sparse textures have
  peaks far above their average level; the tail detector now also follows peak
  energy, so those tails are no longer truncated well above your threshold.
- **No silence is appended.** The asset ends at the last frame that genuinely
  passed the tail test, instead of up to 40 ms later.
- **Per-item measurement matches the delivery.** Item fades and auto-crossfades
  are neutralised before the analysis render, so an item inside a crossfade is
  no longer measured attenuated and then trimmed into real audio.
- **The measured-floor diagnostic is back.** When an item is skipped as silent,
  the reported floor is shown again instead of being silently dropped.

## Requirements

- REAPER 7 (64-bit)
- ReaImGui 0.10.0.5 or newer in the 0.10 API series
- Windows, macOS, or Linux
- SWS Extension optional

## Upgrading

Install over the previous version and re-register only the packaged launcher.
Your settings are preserved; trimming stays on with three render passes, exactly
as before. There is no 3.1.2 or public 3.1.3 — 3.2.0 follows 3.1.1.

The release download contains the product, Inter font license, installation
instructions, a machine-readable manifest, and a separate SHA-256 checksum.
